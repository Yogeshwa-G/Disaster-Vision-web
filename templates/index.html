ABOUT DISASTERVISION

See the change.
Respect the evidence.

DisasterVision is a satellite-image monitoring project built to make environmental change easier to explore. By comparing historical observations of selected locations, it presents visible differences and a simple trend projection in one place.

The goal is clarity: show the data used, explain the calculation, and make the limitations visible alongside the result. The project is a decision-support experiment—not a replacement for scientific assessment or emergency services.
